## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: final square mobile app icon artwork for Tacto, a buttonless music player emphasizing tactile, physically intuitive turntable interaction.
Primary request: Create one distinctive, polished, very simple app icon. Express touching and moving a real record, without showing a hand or any playback controls.
Scene/backdrop: solid warm ivory / cream background filling the entire square edge to edge. No surrounding presentation board, no device mockup, no inset rounded-square tile, no outside margin.
Subject: a large deep forest-green almost black vinyl record seen straight from above, centered with a compact warm brass tonearm touching its surface. The record's several broad, beautifully spaced concentric grooves evoke the whorls of a fingerprint, subtly connecting vinyl and tactile touch without depicting a literal finger. A warm terracotta center label with a tiny spindle hole. The single simplified brass tonearm with a subtle horizontal crosspiece can suggest a lowercase t in the overall emblem, while still feeling like a physical record player's arm. Keep the geometry clean and resolved.
Style/medium: premium modern brand emblem with restrained dimensional material cues: softly beveled vinyl edge, fine brushed brass, gentle shallow soft contact shadows. Primarily bold graphic silhouette, not a detailed photorealistic miniature turntable. Sculptural but calm, crafted and approachable.
Composition/framing: square 1:1, single icon, centered, balanced, main emblem occupies about 76% of the canvas with generous safe space for OS corner masking. Readable at 48 pixels. No full turntable deck.
Color palette: warm cream #F1E5CD background, charcoal forest green #202923 vinyl, muted terracotta #BC7354 center, soft champagne brass #C8B78B arm; these echo the app's existing cream, vinyl, and brass interface.
Lighting/mood: subtle diffuse studio light, warm, quiet, tactile, timeless.
Text: no text, no letters printed, no wordmark, no slogan.
Constraints: one final icon, no grids or multiple variants. No buttons, play triangles, pause bars, skip arrows, knobs, equalizer, music-note symbols, literal hands, sparkles, busy textures, glossy neon, dramatic gradients, watermark. Avoid thin fragile decorative detail. Full opaque square background, no transparency.
